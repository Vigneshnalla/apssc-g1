# CABINET COMMITTEES

The Cabinet works through various committees. These committees are called **Cabinet Committees**.

> **Committee → a smaller group formed to deal with particular matters**

The features, list and functions of these committees are explained below.

## FEATURES OF CABINET COMMITTEES

The following are the features of Cabinet Committees:

### 1. Extra-Constitutional Bodies

Cabinet Committees are **extra-constitutional bodies**, as they are **not mentioned in the Constitution** and are established under the **Rules of Business of the Government of India**.

### 2. Standing and Ad Hoc Committees

They are of two types:

* **Standing Committees** — of a permanent nature.
* **Ad hoc Committees** — of a temporary nature.

The ad hoc committees are constituted from time to time to deal with **special problems**. They are disbanded after their task is completed.

### 3. Created According to Need

The Prime Minister creates Cabinet Committees according to the **needs of the time and situation**.

Hence, their **number, nomenclature, and composition** vary from time to time.

### 4. Varying Membership

Their membership varies from one committee to another.

They usually include only **Cabinet Ministers**.

Sometimes, **non-Cabinet Ministers** can also be appointed as their members.

Further, non-Cabinet Ministers can also attend the meetings of Cabinet Committees as **special invitees**.

### 5. Inclusion of Senior Ministers

They not only include the **Ministers in charge of subjects covered by them** but also include **other senior Ministers**.

### 6. Chairman

They are mostly headed by the **Prime Minister**.

Sometimes, other senior Cabinet Ministers also act as their Chairman.

But, in case the Prime Minister is a member of a committee, he/she **invariably [always] presides over it**.

> **PM is a member → PM chairs the committee.**

### 7. Functions and Decisions

They not only **sort out issues and formulate proposals** for the consideration of the Cabinet, but also **take decisions**.

However, the **Cabinet can review their decisions**.

### 8. Organisational Device

They are an **organisational device** to reduce the **enormous workload of the Cabinet**.

They also facilitate:

* **In-depth examination of policy issues**
* **Effective coordination**

They are based on the principles of:

* **Division of labour**
* **Effective delegation**

## LIST OF CABINET COMMITTEES

At present, the following **8 Cabinet Committees** are functional:

1. **Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs**
2. **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs**
3. **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**
4. **Cabinet Committee on Parliamentary Affairs**
5. **Cabinet Committee on Security**
6. **Cabinet Committee on Accommodation**
7. **Cabinet Committee on Investment and Growth**
8. **Cabinet Committee on Employment and Skill Development**

## FUNCTIONS OF CABINET COMMITTEES

1. **Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs**
   Deals with all policy matters pertaining to **domestic and foreign affairs**.

2. **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs**
   Directs and coordinates governmental activities in the **economic sphere**.

3. **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**
   Decides **higher-level appointments** in the Central Secretariat, Public Enterprises, Banks and Financial Institutions.

4. **Cabinet Committee on Parliamentary Affairs**
   Looks after the **progress of government business in Parliament**.

5. **Cabinet Committee on Security**
   Deals with issues relating to **defence, law and order, internal security and atomic energy**.

6. **Cabinet Committee on Accommodation**
   Deals with issues regarding the **allotment of government accommodation** to Members of Parliament, Central Government employees, other persons and various organisations.

7. **Cabinet Committee on Investment and Growth**
   Deals with policy matters aimed at **accelerating capital inflows, promoting exports, promoting import substitution and improving ease of doing business**.

8. **Cabinet Committee on Employment and Skill Development**
   Deals with issues related to **skill development, enhancing employability of the workforce and increasing women’s workforce participation**.

### Important

Of all the Cabinet Committees, the **Political Affairs Committee** is considered the most powerful and is often described as a **“Super-Cabinet.”**

## GROUPS OF MINISTERS (GoMs)

In addition to Cabinet Committees, several **Groups of Ministers (GoMs)** are constituted to look into different issues and subjects.

* Some GoMs are **empowered to take decisions on behalf of the Cabinet**.
* Others **make recommendations to the Cabinet**.

GoMs have become a useful mechanism for **coordination between different ministries**. They are **ad hoc bodies** formed to make recommendations to the Cabinet on certain **emergent issues and critical problem areas**.

* Ministers whose ministries are connected with the issue are included in the relevant GoM.
* Once its recommendations are finalised, the GoM is **dissolved**.

## Second Administrative Reforms Commission — GoMs

The Second Administrative Reforms Commission (2005–2009) made the following observations and recommendations regarding the working of GoMs:

1. The Commission observed that the **large number of GoMs** made it difficult for many of them to **meet regularly and complete their work**, resulting in **significant delays in dealing with major issues**.

2) The Commission recommended that **GoMs should be used selectively**, with **decision-making authority where necessary** and **fixed time limits** for completing their assigned work, to ensure **effective coordination**.

3. The Commission recommended that GoMs should be **used selectively and effectively**, with a **clear mandate and fixed time limits**, so that they can **resolve issues quickly and coordinate effectively**.
