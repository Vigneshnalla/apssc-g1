## Buddhism — 34 Questions

### A. Buddhist Philosophy and Teachings

1. **Question:** The concept of Anatman (no soul) is a fundamental tenet of which school?

**Answer:** Theravada Buddhism.

**Note:** Anatman (Anatta), the doctrine of no permanent self, is fundamental to Buddhism generally, not exclusive to Theravada.

2. **Question:** What does the core philosophical term Pratityasamutpada (or Paticca-samuppada) signify in Buddhism?

**Answer:** Theory of Dependent Origination.

3. **Question:** The concept of Pratityasamutpada is also commonly known by what other descriptive name?

**Answer:** The 12 Nidanas.

4. **Question:** Which of the options is not one of the Four Noble Truths of Buddha: Life is full of sorrow, The cause of sorrow is desire, Suffering can be removed by following the 10 Commandments, or The path to end sorrow is the Eightfold Path?

**Answer:** Suffering can be removed by following the 10 Commandments.

5. **Question:** The Eightfold Path of Buddhism includes all of the options except which one: Right View, Right Livelihood, Right Prayer, or Right Mindfulness?

**Answer:** Right Prayer.

6. **Question:** Which sect of Buddhism explicitly believes in the divinity of Buddha and worships his idols?

**Answer:** Mahayana Buddhism.

**Note:** This is a traditional exam-oriented distinction. Mahayana emphasizes Bodhisattvas and developed forms of Buddha devotion, but its traditions are diverse.

7. **Question:** Which Buddhist teaching is represented by the 12 Nidanas?

**Answer:** Dependent Origination (Pratityasamutpada).

**Note:** This overlaps with Questions 2 and 3 but is retained from your revised list.

### B. Buddhist Literature and Texts

8. **Question:** Which Buddhist text provides a detailed list of the 16 Mahajanapadas?

**Answer:** Anguttara Nikaya.

9. **Question:** In the context of ancient India, the Therigatha text is a part of which Nikaya?

**Answer:** Khuddaka Nikaya.

10. **Question:** The Dhammapada is a collection of sayings of Buddha in verse form; it is a part of which major basket?

**Answer:** Sutta Pitaka.

11. **Question:** The famous text Milinda Panha is structured as a philosophical dialogue between the Indo-Greek King Menander and which Buddhist monk?

**Answer:** Nagasena.

12. **Question:** What do the ancient Jataka Stories explicitly relate to?

**Answer:** The previous births of Gautama Buddha.

13. **Question:** The sacred Vinaya Pitaka text mainly deals with what aspects?

**Answer:** Rules of monastic discipline.

14. **Question:** Who authored the epic biography Buddha Charitam?

**Answer:** Ashvaghosha (Ashvaghosa).

### C. Buddhist Councils

15. **Question:** Who presided over the historic First Buddhist Council?

**Answer:** Mahakassapa (Mahakashyapa).

**Note:** Traditionally held at Rajagriha under Ajatashatru.

16. **Question:** The Third Buddhist Council was held at Pataliputra under the royal patronage of which king?

**Answer:** Emperor Ashoka.

17. **Question:** Who presided over the celebrated Fourth Buddhist Council held in Kashmir?

**Answer:** Vasumitra.

**Note:** Traditionally associated with King Kanishka.

18. **Question:** In which of the ancient Buddhist councils did the final, definitive compilation of the Abhidhamma Pitaka take place?

**Answer:** Third Buddhist Council.

**Note:** This is the traditional Theravada association; the historical compilation of Abhidhamma literature was gradual.

### D. Life of Gautama Buddha

19. **Question:** What was the original childhood name of Gautama Buddha?

**Answer:** Siddhartha.

20. **Question:** Who among the choices were the teachers of Gautama Buddha before he achieved final enlightenment?

**Answer:** Both Alara Kalama and Uddaka Ramaputta.

21. **Question:** What was the name of Buddha's favourite horse?

**Answer:** Kanthaka.

22. **Question:** Who was the first female disciple of Gautama Buddha?

**Answer:** Maha Prajapati Gautami.

**Note:** Traditionally recognized as the first woman ordained into the Buddhist Sangha.

23. **Question:** Which of the following ancient Indian kings was a contemporary of both Gautama Buddha and Vardhamana Mahavira?

**Answer:** Bimbisara.

24. **Question:** Where did Lord Buddha attain his Mahaparinirvana (physical demise)?

**Answer:** Kushinagar.

25. **Question:** Which ancient language was primarily and systematically used by Buddha for his popular teachings?

**Answer:** Pali (standard competitive-exam answer).

**Note:** The historical Buddha likely taught in vernacular Middle Indo-Aryan languages. It is not established that he exclusively used Pali.

### E. Buddhist Symbols, Mudras and Architecture

26. **Question:** Which symbolic representation specifically represents the Nirvana (enlightenment) of Buddha?

**Answer:** Bodhi Tree.

**Note:** The Bodhi Tree symbolizes enlightenment (Bodhi). Mahaparinirvana, or final passing away, is a different event.

27. **Question:** The famous Lion Capital at Sarnath commemorates which distinct event of Buddha's life?

**Answer:** First Sermon.

**Note:** Sarnath is the site of Buddha's First Sermon; the Lion Capital is an Ashokan pillar capital.

28. **Question:** Which mudra of Buddha symbolises his First Sermon delivered at Sarnath?

**Answer:** Dharmachakra Pravartana Mudra.

29. **Question:** Which historical king is credited with the absolute construction of the Mahabodhi Temple at Bodh Gaya?

**Answer:** Emperor Ashoka.

**Note:** Ashoka is associated with the earliest shrine. The surviving main brick temple was built later.

30. **Question:** The iconic Gandhara School of Art was closely and structurally associated with which Buddhist sect?

**Answer:** Mahayana Buddhism.

**Note:** A common exam association, although Gandhara art was not exclusive to Mahayana.

31. **Question:** The Bhumi Sparsha Mudra of Buddha symbolically represents what event?

**Answer:** Buddha calling the Earth to witness his enlightenment while confronting Mara.

32. **Question:** The royal emblem (Rajamudra) of the Mauryas was influenced by which prominent animal associated with Buddha?

**Answer:** Lion.

**Note:** Lions are prominent in Mauryan and Ashokan symbolism, but the stated connection to a specific Mauryan royal emblem is not firmly established.

### F. Buddhist Sangha and Ceremonies

33. **Question:** The Upasampada ceremony in Buddhism refers to what event?

**Answer:** Entry into monkhood through higher ordination.

34. **Question:** In the Buddhist Sangha, what does the ceremony of Pavarana signify?

**Answer:** At the end of the rainy-season retreat, monks invite fellow monks to point out any faults they have observed.

## Jainism and Heterodox Sects — 26 Questions

### A. Jain Tirthankaras and Life of Mahavira

1. **Question:** Which Jain Tirthankara is universally considered by historians to be a verified historical figure along with Mahavira?

**Answer:** Parshvanatha.

2. **Question:** Who was the 23rd Tirthankara of Jainism?

**Answer:** Parshvanatha.

3. **Question:** Who was the 24th and final Tirthankara of Jainism?

**Answer:** Vardhamana Mahavira.

4. **Question:** At which specific place did Vardhamana Mahavira attain his supreme enlightenment (Kaivalya)?

**Answer:** Jrimbhikagrama (Jrimbhika village), traditionally near the Rijupalika River.

5. **Question:** Vardhamana Mahavira was born into which specific Kshatriya clan?

**Answer:** Jnatrika (Gnatrika) clan.

6. **Question:** Who was the very first disciple of Lord Mahavira?

**Answer:** Jamali (as given in the original question).

**Note:** This is disputed. Jamali is traditionally described as Mahavira's son-in-law and an early disciple. Indrabhuti Gautama is generally recognized as his chief disciple and first Ganadhara.

7. **Question:** Which Jain Tirthankara is iconographically represented by the symbol of a snake (serpent)?

**Answer:** Parshvanatha.

### B. Jain Philosophy and Doctrines

8. **Question:** In Jainism, the concept of Syadvada is literally translated into English as what doctrine?

**Answer:** Doctrine of “Maybe”.

**Note:** More precisely, it is the doctrine of conditional or qualified statements.

9. **Question:** Anekantavada (the doctrine of relative pluralism) is a core theory and pillar philosophy of which religion?

**Answer:** Jainism.

10. **Question:** The concept of the Five Vows (Pancha Maha Vrata) was systematically completed by Mahavira by adding which final vow?

**Answer:** Brahmacharya (Chastity).

11. **Question:** Which option is not a part of the Three Jewels (Triratna) of Jainism: Right Faith, Right Knowledge, Right Conduct, or Right Speech?

**Answer:** Right Speech.

12. **Question:** In Jainism, the state of absolute, perfect knowledge is technically known as what?

**Answer:** Kaivalya (Kevala Jnana).

13. **Question:** In Jain philosophy, what is the precise spiritual meaning of the term Arhat?

**Answer:** A spiritually perfected being who has conquered inner passions and attained omniscience.

14. **Question:** What is the actual textual meaning of the ancient title Nirgrantha used for Jain followers?

**Answer:** One who is free from all bonds.

15. **Question:** The rigorous ascetic practice of Sallekhana is associated with which religious sect?

**Answer:** Jainism.

### C. Jain Literature and Councils

16. **Question:** Who authored the classic Jain text known as the Kalpa Sutra?

**Answer:** Bhadrabahu (traditional attribution).

17. **Question:** Where was the historic Second Jain Council organized and held?

**Answer:** Vallabhi, Gujarat.

18. **Question:** Which text is considered the primary sacred book of the Svetambara Jains?

**Answer:** 12 Angas.

**Note:** The Svetambara canonical literature is broader than the Angas alone.

### D. Jain Sects and Historical Developments

19. **Question:** The primary structural division of Jainism into the Svetambaras and Digambaras occurred during the reign of which emperor?

**Answer:** Chandragupta Maurya.

**Note:** Traditional accounts connect the early division with this period, but the sectarian separation developed over time.

20. **Question:** Which famous ancient Indian king converted to Jainism and walked down to Shravanabelagola in his final days?

**Answer:** Chandragupta Maurya.

**Note:** According to later Jain tradition.

21. **Question:** What historical epoch or era can be best described as the interregnum between the death of Mahavira and the beginning of the Mauryan Empire?

**Answer:** Pre-Mauryan Age of Heterodox Sects.

**Note:** This is a descriptive expression rather than a universally recognized period name.

### E. Jain Architecture, Inscriptions and Sacred Places

22. **Question:** Ancient Jain architecture and structural style is best exemplified by the magnificent temples located at which site?

**Answer:** Dilwara on Mount Abu, Rajasthan.

23. **Question:** The historical term Basadi (or Basti) in South India explicitly refers to what kind of structure?

**Answer:** Jain Temple.

24. **Question:** The famous ancient Hatigumpha Inscription (Elephant Cave inscription) of King Kharavela mentions and praises which religion?

**Answer:** Jainism.

25. **Question:** Which sacred geographic place is common to both Buddhism and Jainism as a highly revered holy site?

**Answer:** All of the above — Rajgir, Shravasti and Vaishali.

### F. Other Heterodox Sects

26. **Question:** Who among the ancient ascetics was the original founder of the fatalist Ajivika sect?

**Answer:** Makkhali Gosala.
