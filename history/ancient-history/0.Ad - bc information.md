# BC, AD, BCE and CE — Historical Dating

## 1. Full Forms and Meanings

| Term | Full form             | Meaning                                                                                |
| ---- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BC   | Before Christ         | Years before the traditional starting point associated with the birth of Jesus Christ. |
| AD   | *Anno Domini* (Latin) | “In the year of the Lord”; used for years in the era beginning with AD 1.              |
| BCE  | Before Common Era     | The same years as BC, expressed using a religiously neutral term.                      |
| CE   | Common Era            | The same years as AD, expressed using a religiously neutral term.                      |

**Important:** BC and BCE refer to the same years. AD and CE also refer to the same years. The dates and numbers remain unchanged.

## 2. Why Do We Use Two Different Terms?

BC/AD and BCE/CE represent the same dating system, but they use different terminology.

* **BC/AD:** Uses terminology associated with Christianity.

* **BCE/CE:** Uses religiously neutral terminology  and is widely used in academic, scientific, archaeological, and historical writing.

[ Religiously neutral terminology means using words that do not favour or refer specifically to one religion. ]

**Example:** 500 BC = 500 BCE, and AD 500 = 500 CE.

The main difference is the terminology, not the dates themselves.

## 3. How Do We Read the Timeline?

### Earlier in history

**500 BCE → 200 BCE → 1 BCE**

BCE years count backwards as time moves towards the Common Era. Therefore, a larger BCE number represents an older date.

### Later in history

**1 CE → 200 CE → 500 CE**

CE years count forwards. Therefore, a larger CE number represents a more recent date.

**Important:** There is no year zero in the conventional BC/AD or BCE/CE dating system. The sequence goes directly from **1 BCE to 1 CE**.

## 4. Important Examples

* 500 BCE = 500 BC.

* 2026 CE = AD 2026.

* 500 BCE is older than 200 BCE.

* 500 CE is later than 200 CE.

* 1 BCE is immediately followed by 1 CE; there is no year zero.

## 5. How to Calculate Centuries

| Century              | Years covered |
| -------------------- | ------------- |
| 1st century BCE      | 100–1 BCE     |
| 1st century CE / AD  | 1–100 CE      |
| 2nd century CE / AD  | 101–200 CE    |
| 5th century CE / AD  | 401–500 CE    |
| 21st century CE / AD | 2001–2100 CE  |

**Exam trap:** The 21st century began on 1 January 2001, not 1 January 2000.

### Quick Revision

* **BC = BCE** — same years.

* **AD = CE** — same years.

* BCE numbers decrease as time moves towards 1 BCE.

* CE numbers increase as time moves forward.

* There is no year zero.

* **c.** means *circa*, or approximately, when used before a historical date.
